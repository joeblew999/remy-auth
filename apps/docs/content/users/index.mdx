**For people using the Remy app.** Building it, or calling its API? That is the
[developer docs](/dev).

Remy works in thirteen languages, right to left as well as left to right, and shows dates, numbers and
money the way each language writes them. Sign in with a code sent to your email, and keep notes you can
share with other people.
