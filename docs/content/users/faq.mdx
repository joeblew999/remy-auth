<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Which languages are there?">
    Thirteen: English, Spanish, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Thai, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Hindi,
    Amharic, Polish, Turkish and German.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the page read right to left?">
    Arabic, Persian and Hebrew are written right to left, so the whole page turns with them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work without JavaScript?">
    The site's pages and this guide do. The app needs JavaScript.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask my AI assistant about Remy?">
    Yes. Add this guide to your assistant as an MCP server,
    `https://remy-auth-docs.gedw99.workers.dev/api/mcp/docs`, or give it
    `https://remy-auth-docs.gedw99.workers.dev/docs/llms.txt`. Each page's **Open** menu also opens it
    in ChatGPT or Claude.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
