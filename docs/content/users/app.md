The app opens from **:ui[open_app]** in the top bar.

![The Remy app](/media/app.png)

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| :ui[nav_home] | Where you are in the app, and what each page is for |
| :ui[nav_formats] | Dates, numbers and money as your language writes them ([Formats](./formats.mdx)) |
| :ui[nav_clock] | The time now where you are and in the time zones you add; the address keeps them, so you can share it |
| :ui[nav_account] | Signing in with a code sent to your email, and your account once you are in |
| :ui[nav_notes] | Notes you write and share with other people, as readers or editors |
| :ui[nav_demo] | A counter and a reservation form, to try the app |
| :ui[nav_location] | Your time zone and region, as your browser reports them |
| :ui[nav_settings] | Your language and light or dark appearance, and what your device tells the app |

## Getting around

- **On a phone**, the bar at the bottom holds :ui[nav_home], :ui[nav_formats], :ui[nav_clock] and :ui[nav_account].
  **:ui[nav_more]** opens every page, the :ui[nav_guide] and the way back to the site.
- **On a tablet or computer**, the list of pages is down the side. The button at the top left shrinks it
  to icons and back.

Developers: how the app is built is in [the developer docs](/dev/run-and-test).
