The app opens from **Open the app** in the top bar.

![The Remy app](/media/app.png)

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Where you are in the app, and what each page is for |
| Formats | The same values as the site's [Formats](./formats.mdx) page, inside the app |
| Location | Your time zone and region, as your browser reports them |
