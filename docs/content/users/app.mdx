The app opens from **Open the app** in the top bar.

![The Remy app](/media/app.png)

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Where you are in the app, and what each page is for |
| Formats | The same values as the site's [Formats](./formats.mdx) page, inside the app |
| Clock | The time now where you are and in the time zones you add; the address keeps them, so you can share it |
| Account | Your sign-in, once signing in arrives |
| Demo | A counter and a reservation form, to try the app |
| Location | Your time zone and region, as your browser reports them |
| Settings | Your language and light or dark appearance, and what your device tells the app |

## Getting around

- **On a phone**, the bar at the bottom holds Overview, Formats, Clock and Account. **More** opens every
  page, the guide and the way back to the site.
- **On a tablet or computer**, the list of pages is down the side. The button at the top left shrinks it
  to icons and back.

Developers: how the app is built is in [the developer docs](/dev/gui).
